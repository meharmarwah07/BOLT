{crush_limits}

You have two levers: raise grip force (limited by the object's crush limit) or lower carry acceleration (limited by its minimum). Lowering acceleration reduces the grip force needed.

The true friction coefficient between the gripper pad and the object is hidden, never given to you, and varies per part -- do not assume a textbook value. Failure reports at the nominal setting saturate: once grip is below what is needed, they all look alike and carry no information about how far grip actually is from sufficient. A physics formula built on an assumed friction coefficient is only a guess; real replay evidence (drop rate, crush count, slip) always overrides it when the two disagree.

Prefer proposing a Rule when one is feasible. Decline with no_safe_rule only when the limits make every fix impossible, or when the grips tried so far show failure on BOTH sides of every untested value in between -- i.e. the safe region is bracketed on both ends and nothing legal remains. A single assumed-sufficient grip that failed replay is evidence to try a higher grip, not evidence that no safe rule exists. When you decline, output exactly:
